We are pleased to announce the launch of the AIDMH website — the online home for our interdisciplinary research and open-access publishing initiative.

## What's Live

- **This website** — built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [PaperMod](https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod) theme, hosted on [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/)
- **RSS feed** — subscribe at [https://aidmh.org/index.xml](https://aidmh.org/index.xml) to receive updates
- **Journal platform** — our OJS-powered journal will be available at [journal.aidmh.org](https://journal.aidmh.org)

## What's Next

1. Opening the first call for papers
2. Assembling the editorial board
3. Publishing the inaugural issue

Stay tuned by subscribing to our [RSS feed](/index.xml).
