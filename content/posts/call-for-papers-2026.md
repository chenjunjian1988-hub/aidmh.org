AIDMH is now accepting submissions for its **inaugural issue**, to be published on our OJS platform at [journal.aidmh.org](https://journal.aidmh.org).

## Topics

We welcome original research articles, review papers, and case studies across all disciplines, with particular interest in:

- Interdisciplinary and cross-domain research
- Open science and reproducible research
- Digital humanities and computational methods
- Public health and medical research

## Submission Guidelines

1. Register at [journal.aidmh.org](https://journal.aidmh.org)
2. Follow the author submission workflow
3. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review

## Important Dates

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
