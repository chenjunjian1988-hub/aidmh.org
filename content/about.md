## About AIDMH

AIDMH is an interdisciplinary research initiative dedicated to advancing open-access scholarly publishing. Our mission is to provide a platform for high-quality research across multiple disciplines, ensuring that knowledge is freely accessible to researchers, practitioners, and the public worldwide.

## Open Access Commitment

We believe that research should be freely available to all. Our publications follow open-access principles, with no subscription barriers or paywalls. All published content is available under Creative Commons licenses.

## Journal Platform

Our primary journal platform is hosted at [journal.aidmh.org](https://journal.aidmh.org), powered by Open Journal Systems (OJS). Authors are invited to submit manuscripts through the journal portal.
